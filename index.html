KNOW THE THREATS

Common Cyber Crimes

🎣

Phishing

Fraudulent messages or websites that trick users into revealing confidential information.

💳

Online Fraud

Deceptive online activities intended to obtain money or sensitive information.

👤

Impersonation

Pretending to be another person or organization to deceive a victim.

🔐

Account Compromise

Unauthorized access to an account through stolen credentials or social engineering.

PROTECT YOURSELF

Cyber Safety Tips

01

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts.

02

Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication wherever available.

03

Never Share OTPs

Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.

04

Check Links

Verify suspicious links and messages before clicking.

05

Update Devices

Keep software and security updates installed.

06

Report Incidents

Report suspicious activity through appropriate official channels.

WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

What Should You Do?

  1. Stop communicating with the suspicious account.
  2. Preserve screenshots, messages and transaction details.
  3. Secure your account and change compromised passwords.
  4. Inform your bank/service provider if money is involved.
  5. Report through the appropriate official cyber crime channel.
